My Shadow Text

On the way home from school, / Ari heard a whimper / behind the mailboxes. A small brown dog / was shivering in the rain, / his fur caked with mud. He wore a red collar, / but the tag was gone. “Somebody must be missing you,” / Ari whispered, / and the dog licked his hand. Ari carried him home / and called for his mom / before he even took off his boots. Together they dried the dog / with an old towel / and gave him a bowl of water. “We can't keep him,” / Mom said gently. / “Let's help him find his way home.” First, they took him to the vet / to check for a microchip, / but he didn't have one. Next, Ari made posters / with a photo and the words / “Found: One Muddy Dog.” They taped them to lampposts, / and Mom posted the photo / on the neighborhood website. That night, / the dog slept by Ari's bed, / and Ari wondered if anyone would call. Early the next morning, / a voice on the phone said, / “I think you found Biscuit! / He slipped out of our yard / three days ago!” When a girl named Rosa / came through the door, / Biscuit raced to her, / wagging his whole body. Ari felt a little sad to say goodbye, / but mostly he felt proud. Rosa smiled. / “Will you come and visit him?” / Ari nodded hard.

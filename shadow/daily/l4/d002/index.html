My Shadow Text

On his first day at Maple School, / Jun held his lunch box / very tightly. Inside were rolls of gimbap / that his mom had made / that morning. At lunchtime, / a girl at his table / pointed at his food. “What is that?” / she asked. Jun's cheeks turned hot. / He wanted to hide the box / under the table. “It's... / gimbap,” / he said quietly. / “Rice and vegetables / rolled in seaweed.” The girl's eyes went wide. / “Seaweed? / That's so cool!” Her name was Ava, / and she had never tried it before. Jun took a deep breath / and offered her a piece. Ava chewed / and nodded. / “Mmm! / It's salty / and a little bit sweet!” The next day, / Ava brought her grandma's empanadas / to share with Jun. Soon, / their whole table was sharing bites / and telling stories / about their families' favorite foods. Jun still held his lunch box / on the way to school— / but now he held it / proudly.

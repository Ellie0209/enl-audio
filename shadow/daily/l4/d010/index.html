My Shadow Text

High above the town, / inside a gray cloud, / a tiny snowflake named Flurry / was born. She had six sparkling arms, / and no other snowflake / looked exactly like her. All around her, / her sisters and brothers / began to drift down. “Come on, Flurry!” / they called. / “It's time to fall!” But Flurry clung to the cloud. / “What if I land / somewhere cold and lonely?” An old raindrop floated by. / “I have fallen many times,” / he said gently. “Once I landed on a rose. / Once I filled a puddle / for a little bird to drink.” “Every landing / is a new adventure.” Flurry took a deep breath / and let go. She twirled past rooftops / and danced around a streetlight. Finally, / she landed softly / on the tip of a little boy's nose. The boy giggled / and looked up at the sky. / “It's snowing!” / he shouted. Flurry melted into a tiny drop, / but she wasn't sad. “Now I'm a raindrop,” / she thought. / “Where will my next adventure take me?”

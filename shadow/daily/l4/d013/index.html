My Shadow Text

On Saturday morning, / a huge puddle sat / in the middle of our driveway. My little brother, Eli, / sailed a leaf boat across it / until lunchtime. But by three o'clock, / the puddle had shrunk / to the size of a pizza. By dinner, / it had vanished completely! “Someone stole our puddle!” / Eli shouted. I grabbed my notebook / and became Detective Jada. First, I checked for footprints. / There were none. Next, I looked for a drain nearby, / but the driveway was flat and solid. Finally, I asked the smartest scientist I know: / my grandma. “The sun was the thief,” / she said with a wink. “Its heat turned the water / into an invisible gas / called water vapor. That's called evaporation. / The vapor floated up / into the air.” “Will it ever come back?” / Eli asked, / looking worried. Grandma pointed at the clouds. / “Someday it will fall again / as rain.” I wrote in my notebook: / Case closed. / The puddle is just on vacation!

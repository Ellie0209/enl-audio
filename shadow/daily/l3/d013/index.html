My Shadow Text

Listen to my clues, / and guess who I am! I have a very long neck / and spots all over my body. I eat leaves from the tops of tall trees. / What am I? I'm a giraffe! / My tongue is dark purple / and almost as long as your arm. I live in the rainforest / and move very, very slowly. I hang from branches / and sleep for many hours a day. / What am I? I'm a sloth! / I'm so slow / that tiny green plants can grow on my fur. I'm a tiny bird, / about as long as your finger. My wings flap so fast / that they make a humming sound. / What am I? I'm a hummingbird! / I can even fly backward! Now it's your turn. / Can you make a riddle / about your favorite animal?

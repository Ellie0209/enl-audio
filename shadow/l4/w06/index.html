My Shadow Text

On Monday morning, / the school's soccer trophy / was gone. The glass case was open, / and there were muddy footprints on the floor. Principal Diaz frowned. / “Who would do this?” Mia pulled out her notebook. / Detective time. Clue one: / The footprints were small and round— / not shoes. Clue two: / A trail of crumbs / led out the back door. Clue three: / The custodian said / the door had been open all night / to air out the gym. Mia followed the crumbs / across the field / to the old shed. She peeked inside— / and laughed. Curled up in the trophy / was a raccoon, / fast asleep, / surrounded by cookie wrappers. “Our thief,” / Mia announced, / “has four paws / and a sweet tooth.” The custodian gently carried the raccoon / back to the woods. The trophy got a good cleaning— / and Mia got a detective badge.

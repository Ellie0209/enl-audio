My Shadow Text

Elsie lived with her father / in a lighthouse on a rocky island. Every night, / her father climbed the stairs / to light the great lamp, / so ships would not crash on the rocks. One stormy evening, / her father slipped on the wet steps / and hurt his ankle. “The lamp,” / he groaned. / “Someone must light it.” Elsie looked at the ninety-nine steps / spiraling up into the dark. The wind howled. / Rain hammered the windows. She took a deep breath / and climbed. At the top, / her hands were shaking, / but she struck the match / just like she had seen a hundred times. The lamp flared. / A beam of light / swept across the black sea. Far out in the waves, / a small fishing boat / turned away from the rocks / just in time. The next morning, / the fishermen rowed to the island / with a basket of fresh bread / and a note: / “Thank you, Lighthouse Keeper.” Elsie's father smiled. / “They mean you.”
